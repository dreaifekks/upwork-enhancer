Upwork Enhancer Settings

Tune how jobs are scored. Settings are stored in this browser.

Profile

Short context used by scoring and optional AI.

Matching

Preferred skills and project types raise the match score; avoided ones lower it.

Budget floor

Jobs priced below these amounts lose match points.

Risk phrases

A blacklisted phrase always recommends Pass. Off-platform phrases lower the risk score.

Scoring weights

Relative importance of each dimension. Values are normalized to 100%.

Action thresholds

Overall score needed for each recommendation. Keep Apply ≥ Watch ≥ Pass.

AI assistance

Optional. When you start an AI action, the job details, your profile summary, and matching answer templates are sent to the endpoint you configure.
